Political Relationship Web

Radial spider-web of political relationships. The party hub sits at the centre, its highest-volume members form the inner ring, and connected figures — other-party members, executive branch and businesses — fan out on the outer rings. Edges merge a curated overlay (business deals, appointments, exec-branch ties) with edges derived from the live database (shared stocks, co-timed trades, shared sectors, shared committees, leadership ties, same-state delegations).
